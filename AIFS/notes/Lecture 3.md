Evans Pritchard worked in Eastern Africa which was under a British colony back in 20s, 30s and 40s. He was one of the leading social anthropologists at the time. Most people in anthropology were thinking in linear evolutionary terms (we will return to this later on). They assumed that people in different countries, different groups sat along linear lines of development from primitive to oxford/cambridge professors.
Evans was interested in the mental life. He thought that primitive people WERE capable of science reasoning because most people that you show science manage to understand it. So he went there and started talking to them (which most anthropologists back then didn't do) and he came across these wooden structures. When he asked them, they knew perfectly well the reason.
